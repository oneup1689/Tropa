# Privacy policy / Политика конфиденциальности

Tropa does not have accounts, servers, analytics, ads or trackers. The developer does not receive your data.

## What stays on your phone
Your tracks (coordinates, time, altitude, speed), notes, photo thumbnails, routes and settings are stored only in the app's local database on your device.
Photos and videos you attach to notes are saved to your phone gallery, not to the app.
The app does not upload any of it. You choose when to export or share a GPX file, a poster, or a backup.

## Requests to the internet (all optional)
| When | Service | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Downloading a map region | build.protomaps.com | Which map tiles you request (a region you chose) |
| Creating a poster: weather | Open-Meteo | Approximate coordinates and date of the walk |
| Creating a poster: elevation | OpenTopoData, Open-Meteo | Coordinates of sampled track points |
| Creating a poster: place name | BigDataCloud, Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) | Coordinates of the walk |

These services see your IP address and the data above, under their own privacy policies. Recording and viewing walks never use the internet.
The app has an optional Android cloud backup switch (Settings, Privacy), off by default; when you turn it on, the database is copied to your own Google account by Android.

## Permissions
- Location, including background location: to record a walk with the screen off.
- Notifications and foreground service: to show that recording is running and to alert you if GPS is turned off.
- Physical activity: to start auto-record when you begin walking.
- Battery optimisation exemption, exact alarms, boot completed: to keep recording alive and to run scheduled walks.
- Internet and network state: only for the requests listed above.

## Deleting data
Delete a walk in the app, or uninstall the app: everything stored by the app is removed. Files you exported or photos in the gallery are yours and stay.
